ANIMATION

RAMEN:
A JOURNEY TO JAPAN

CLICK FOR VIDEO

This is an animation project video that was assigned for me and my team. We created with an educational theme video. The main topic is about ramen, including its origin and a tutorial on how to cook ramen in English. This project was entirely created in After Effects, with all illustrations made in Procreate. I was in charge for all of the assets quality in this video including creating most of the illustrations, creating motion graphics, SFX, and etc.

The video not only provides a step-by-step tutorial on how to cook ramen but also delves into a brief history of ramen, exploring its origins and cultural significance.
