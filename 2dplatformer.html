CHARACTER DESIGN

ARLO
ARLO is an alien explorer equipped with a sleek, high-tech spacesuit designed for space adventures. The suit is lightweight yet protective, featuring glowing panels that shift colors based on the environment or ARLO’s emotions. Its transparent helmet provides adaptive vision for different planetary conditions.

ARLO’s most striking feature is their skin, which has a unique fabric-like texture. The surface appears woven with shimmering, iridescent threads, marking them as an otherworldly being. This "fabric" texture hints at a biological function, such as camouflage or energy absorption.
ENEMY TARANTULA
A deadly alien tarantula with glowing eyes and spindly legs, it patrols its territory with precision, scaling walls and ceilings. Tough and venomous, it can camouflage itself to ambush players. Relentless and adaptable, it’s a constant threat in any environment.

The alien tarantula patrols the platform, moving with eerie precision as it tracks the player's every move. It waits for the right moment to strike, closing in when the player lets their guard down, always ready to attack at a moment’s notice.
