Rendang Mama Hanny

I utilized a vibrant red and yellow color palette as the main visual theme for this packaging design, reflecting the rich and warm essence of the products. To enhance the brand identity, I crafted a custom logo for Rendang Mama Hanny. This logo features an illustration of Mama Hanny's face, adding a personal and authentic touch. The design also incorporates a detailed image of rendang as the focal point, ensuring that the packaging effectively communicates the delicious and traditional nature of the product.

Semur Mama Hanny

I designed the packaging for Semur Mama Hanny with a carefully chosen base color palette of blue and yellow, aiming to create a fresh, inviting, and comforting feel that resonates with the product’s traditional roots. The color combination not only enhances the visual appeal but also conveys a sense of warmth and trust
