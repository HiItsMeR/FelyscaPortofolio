LANDING PAGE
FOR AR META FILTER

HOVER ME

In this project, I was assigned individually as my college assignment to create a sustainavle form of design. And I decidedd to create a Meta augmented reality (AR) filter and designed a supporting landing page entirely in Figma. The landing page provides detailed information about the AR content, helping users understand and engage with the filter more effectively.

In this project, I created two versions of an AR filter: 1. "Vitality in Simplicity" – This filter features an image of a skull that appears to be slowly reviving as it waters itself, leading to the growth of flowers and plants around it, creating a more vibrant environment. 2. "Fruit of Knowledge" – This filter showcases a snake reaching for the "Fruit of Knowledge," which contains a cocoon that is on the verge of hatching into a butterfly.
